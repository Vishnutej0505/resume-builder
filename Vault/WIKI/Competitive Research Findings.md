# Competitive Research Findings

Real evidence found before committing to this idea:

- A confirmed India-specific complaint: a user paid **₹850/month** for an existing resume app and still got a watermarked, feature-locked resume. Dark patterns are common in this category — ad-gated exports (3 free exports after 300 ad views), fake trials.
- One competitor resume/CV app is estimated at **~600K downloads/month** — proves the top-of-funnel demand is real and large.
- Global competitor **Novoresume** charges $21.99/month-$139.99/year — not localized for India, part of the general pattern where global apps overcharge relative to Indian income.

This evidence is what justified building in this category despite it being crowded — see [[Undercut pricing thought]] for why "crowded but with a verified real complaint" was treated differently from just guessing a category is overpriced.
