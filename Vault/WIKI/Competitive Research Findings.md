# Competitive Research Findings

Real evidence found before committing to this idea:

- A confirmed India-specific complaint: a user paid **₹850/month** for an existing resume app and still got a watermarked, feature-locked resume. Dark patterns are common in this category — ad-gated exports (3 free exports after 300 ad views), fake trials.
- One competitor resume/CV app is estimated at **~600K downloads/month** — proves the top-of-funnel demand is real and large.
- Global competitor **Novoresume** charges $21.99/month-$139.99/year — not localized for India, part of the general pattern where global apps overcharge relative to Indian income.
- **Intelligent CV - Resume Builder** (Play Store): 50M+ downloads, 4.5★/535K reviews, ad-supported, "500+ templates in 15 colors" marketing angle, 13MB. This is the template-mill end of the market — breadth of templates as the pitch, not rewrite/tailoring quality or export honesty. Confirms the volume this category can reach, and that "many templates" alone is already a saturated, undifferentiated pitch — reinforces that this app's wedge (honest pricing + AI tailoring quality, not template count) is the right differentiator, not a me-too template count race.

This evidence is what justified building in this category despite it being crowded — see [[Undercut pricing thought]] for why "crowded but with a verified real complaint" was treated differently from just guessing a category is overpriced.

## Update: the "honest pricing" wedge is no longer unique (refreshed research)

A live web search (not just the original anecdotal evidence above) found a wave of newer competitors — **Infinite Resume, DuckResume, JobScoutly** — whose entire pitch is now "free forever, no watermark, AI-powered," the same fight this app's wedge picked. **Novoresume** ($20-24/month to remove its watermark) also ships a real ATS score + free "AI Resume Job Matcher" (score + keyword gaps, no account needed). **Internshala** is the closest India-specific competitor: free, AI-assisted, fresher-focused, but with no transparent scoring or JD-targeted full rewrite.

What still holds up as genuinely differentiated after this research:
1. **Full Rewrite as a one-tap action** (paste a JD → every bullet retargeted at once, reviewed individually, discardable) — not found in any competitor checked; Novoresume's job matcher analyzes gaps but doesn't auto-rewrite the whole resume in one action.
2. **Transparent, explainable ATS checklist** — most competitors show an opaque score; this app shows exactly which checks passed/failed.
3. **India-specific rupee pricing** — the new free-tier US entrants have no localized pricing at all; Internshala is India-focused but lacks 1 and 2.

Conclusion: "free export, no watermark" alone is table stakes now, not a moat — the real differentiator going forward is Full Rewrite quality + transparent scoring + India pricing, together, not the honesty framing by itself.
