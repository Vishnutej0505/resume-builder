# Undercut pricing thought

Early instinct: "find an app that's overpriced and undersell it." Applied generally across a few categories before landing on this app specifically.

What actually held up under checking: the resume builder category had a *verified* real complaint (₹850/month, still watermarked) — not just an assumption that existing apps are expensive. That real complaint is what justified the pricing/positioning decision, not the general "undercut" instinct on its own.
