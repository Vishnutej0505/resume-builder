# Home — Resume Builder Vault

This vault is scoped to one project: the India-focused, AI-assisted **Resume Builder** app. Everything here should trace back to that.

## The method

Notes that pile up are a graveyard. Notes that talk to each other are a mind. Keep writing atomic, single-idea notes and link them liberally with [[wikilinks]] instead of relying on folders to do the organizing.

## The three folders

- [[RAW Overview]] — half-formed thoughts, rejected paths, and open questions about this app, dumped as they come up.
- [[WIKI Overview]] — the locked, structured decisions and reasoning behind this app.
- [[OUTPUT Overview]] — what's actually been built and shipped so far.
